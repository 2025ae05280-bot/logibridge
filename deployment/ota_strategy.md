# OTA strategy

At the assignment's 280 KB model size, one full transfer to 85 trucks is 23.8 MB and costs ₹2.38 at ₹0.10/MB (decimal MB). A 10-truck canary costs ₹0.28 for its initial transfer; transferring the remaining 75 trucks after the gate costs ₹2.10, so a successful complete rollout still transfers 23.8 MB and costs ₹2.38. A rollback may require another transfer to the canary cohort. Shadow mode also transfers the candidate model to all 85 trucks (₹2.38 per cycle) and adds comparison-log traffic whose size must be measured rather than guessed. These figures use the brief's planning size; final report calculations should replace it with model sizes in `optimisation/results/benchmark_results.csv`.

Canary is the default for a safety system: deploy to 10 trucks, verify Critical recall, connectivity, and watchdog health, then expand. It limits the first failure domain and requires only 2.8 MB before the first gate. Full replacement is operationally simple but sends an unverified model to every truck at once. Shadow mode is valuable for comparing a candidate during rural coverage gaps, but does not itself authorize the model to control alerts; it also has no lower model-transfer cost than a full rollout. Every strategy retains on-truck inference and queued local alerts, so a disconnected cellular uplink cannot block the safety decision.
